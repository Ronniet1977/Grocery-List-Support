Privacy Policy

Grocery List for Moms

Grocery List for Moms is designed to help users create and manage grocery lists and share grocery items with family members.

Information We Collect

Grocery List for Moms does not sell personal information.

Grocery list information may be stored using Apple iCloud and CloudKit so that lists can sync across devices and be shared with family members.

iCloud and CloudKit

Data stored through iCloud and CloudKit is handled by Apple according to Apple's privacy policies and security practices.

Third-Party Stores

Grocery List for Moms may allow users to select preferred retailers such as Walmart, Target, Kroger, Food Lion, Instacart, or other stores. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by these retailers unless specifically stated.

Data Sharing

Grocery list information is shared only when the user chooses to use the app's family sharing features.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as the app changes.
